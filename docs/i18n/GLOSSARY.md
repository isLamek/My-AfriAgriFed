# AfriAgriFed glossary

The words the site uses again and again, and how each one is written in every language.
A translator should use the same word every time, so a farmer sees "order" and "order" and
never "order" then "request" for the same thing.

How to use it: when you review a translation (see `docs/I18N.md`), search the spreadsheet for
the English word, and check the other languages' column here. If you change a word, change it
everywhere and tell the team, then update this table.

Words kept in English in every language: **AfriAgriFed**, **AAF**, **N$**, **WhatsApp**, and the
names of places, towns and regions (Oshana, Omusati, Oshakati...).

## Things translators should know

- Text with `{name}`, `{count}`, `{list}` and similar in curly braces: keep each one exactly as
  written; they are filled in by the site (a person's name, a number, a product). Their order
  in the sentence may change.
- Words with `_one` / `_other` come as a pair: the first is used for exactly 1, the second for
  every other number.
- `<strong>`-style emphasis is not written in the text; where a word is bold on screen the
  sentence has a `{placeholder}` for it.
- Do not translate what members typed (names, post text, product names): the site shows it as
  written. Only the site's own words are translated.
- Legal pages (Terms, Privacy, Cookie policy, Refund policy) and the admin area are English
  only for now; they need a qualified legal translator.
